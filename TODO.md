# Cashmere Events — outcome checklist

## 1. High-impact hero and 2026 booking message
- The site introduces Cashmere Events as a Nairobi luxury wedding and event styling business.
- The hero communicates Kenya-wide positioning, soft rustic and elegant setups, and 2026 booking availability.
- The hero has prominent WhatsApp and consultation calls to action.

## 2. Services and selected work
- The site presents core luxury wedding and event styling offerings in an elegant, easy-to-scan format.
- A responsive editorial gallery showcases premium event setups with accessible image presentation.

## 3. Process and social proof
- The site explains the client journey from initial consultation through concept development, styling, and event-day execution.
- The site includes testimonials and trust-focused messaging for couples and clients across Kenya.

## 4. Booking and contact conversion
- The site includes a consultation request form with accessible labels and a clear success state.
- Phone numbers are WhatsApp-friendly links using the provided booking numbers.
- The contact area includes phone numbers, social links, Nairobi/service-location messaging, and clear ways to enquire.

## 5. Motion, accessibility, and responsiveness
- The implementation is responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- Motion graphics and micro-interactions are expressive but restrained, with visible focus states and reduced-motion support.
